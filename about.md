I'm Matteo, a computer science student in Cagliari. I'm about 70% made of
movies and games, to borrow my favorite line from Hideo Kojima.

In October 2026 I built [Frogger 3D](https://github.com/SolidusRam/Frogger3D)
in twelve days: a frog that punches cars into each other while a wildfire
chases it. 130 commits, about 15,200 lines of GDScript, 57 models and 71
sound effects generated from code, all made together with an AI using natural language. I also
tried to put it in the browser, watched it crawl at 1-3 frames per second,
and gave up on it. [Read how it went.](/2026/10/frogger-3d-twelve-days/)

I use AI every day, and that's why I'm wary of people who talk about it and
discriminate against the work of others without ever having built something
real with it. What I believe is that automatic programming, done well, lets
anyone who wants to make an impact produce their own software. It doesn't
mean the AI thinks for you. It's the role antirez describes in
["Being Linux Torvalds"](http://antirez.com/news/171): the developer as the
director of a team, not someone who accepts whatever comes out.
